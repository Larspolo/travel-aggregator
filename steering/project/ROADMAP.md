# Roadmap

The goals of travel-aggregator and their features: done (`[x]`) or missing (`[ ]`). The `roadmap` skill makes the next epic from a missing feature.

The source of the goals: the project specification of the owner (2026-11-06), milestones 1 to 5. The MVP is
milestones 1 to 3: `capture:skeleton` to `capture:bahn`, `rank:base` to `rank:profiles`, `trip:list` and `trip:table`.

## 1. Capture offers while I browse

- [x] `capture:skeleton` (#2) Walking skeleton: `docker compose up` (app + db), Alembic migrations at start, an API token, `POST /api/ingest` with normalized offers, the offers in a list on the trip page
  - `docker compose up` starts `app` and `db`, and `GET /api/health` answers "ok"
  - A POST with a valid token shows its offers on the trip page; a request with a bad token gets 401
  - Each green CI on `main` pushes the image `:preview` (job `preview` of `.github/workflows/ci.yml`), and `travel-preview.larspolo.nl` runs it within 5 minutes (`deploy/preview/`); `/api/health` shows its commit (`GIT_SHA`)
- [x] `capture:upsert` (#25) Idempotent upsert by itinerary fingerprint, price history, the raw capture stored compressed, the counts in the response

- [x] `capture:manual` (#117) Manual entry form for offers from sites with no userscript

- [x] `capture:bahn` (#151) bahn.de: JSON interception, server parser, trimmed fixtures, fail-soft parsing, "reparse capture"

- [x] `capture:ns` (#210) NS International: userscript, server parser, trimmed fixtures

- [ ] `capture:queue` (#1663) The userscript panel shows the captures that wait in the queue (`capture:userscript`): their count and the age of the oldest, also in auto-send mode, with "Send now" and "Discard the queue" in the menu; the queue keeps at most 20 captures and drops the oldest with a message (P3: a long failure of the server must not fill the storage of Tampermonkey with no sign; P6: the panel shows it, no browser dialog)

- [ ] `capture:sbb` SBB (sbb.ch): userscript, server parser, trimmed fixtures (P3: the JSON responses of the connections and their fares; the Half Fare card of the travelers as a discount card of `capture:discount`, where the payload tells it), so that the trips to Switzerland (for example Zürich or Basel, `trip:alternatives`) have a Swiss source

- [ ] `capture:seats-left` The seats left of a fare: the parsers store the count where the payload tells it (eurostar.com `fares[].seats`, flixbus.com `available.seats`; no count is no mark), each capture updates it, and the trip page and the pinned page (`trip:pinned-page`) show "N seats left" on a fare with 5 or fewer (P1: only the captured payloads; P3: a cheap fare that is almost sold out shows it before the next search)
  - A Eurostar capture with `"seats": 2` on the Standard fare shows "2 seats left" on that fare, and the fare with 28 seats shows no mark; a later capture with 9 seats removes the mark

- [ ] `capture:resend` A capture that the userscript sends again (a retry after a lost answer of the server, `capture:userscript`) is stored once: the userscript gives each payload a random key, the server keeps one capture per key and token, and a second POST with the same key gets the result of the first with no new capture and no new price row (P3: a slow network must not double the price history, the captures page or the counts of `capture:health`; P4: a PostgreSQL `UNIQUE` constraint)
  - The same payload sent twice with one key gives 1 capture and 1 price row, and the second answer has the counts of the first
  - A payload with no key is stored as before (the older userscripts)
- [x] `capture:redact` (#1901) "Download for a bug report" on the raw view of a capture (`capture:raw-view`): the JSON with the personal values replaced by `"REDACTED"` (names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, discount card numbers, booking codes, and the query values of the URL that are not the search), by a list of keys per parser, so that the owner can attach a failed payload to an issue for a parser fix (P5: no personal data leaves the server by a fix; P3: the payload of a site change reaches the agents)
  - The bug-report download of a bahn.de capture with a traveler name and a BahnCard number gives a file in which both are `"REDACTED"`, the connections and prices are the same, and its upload (`capture:upload`) gives the same offers as the original
- [x] `capture:source-wins` (#1939) Which sources give the best offers: the captures page shows, for the trips of the last 12 months, the number of trips in which the best offer by the default profile has its main fare (the fare with the lowest effective cost) from that source, and the number of trips in which the source has a capture, so that the owner knows which sites to search first (P1: only the captured offers; P2: the score of the default profile)
  - With 5 trips, bahn.de gives the best offer of 3 trips and has captures in 5, and Trainline gives the best offer of 2 trips and has captures in 4: the page shows "bahn.de: best in 3 of 5 trips" and "Trainline: best in 2 of 4 trips"; a source with no capture in the last 12 months shows no row
- [x] `capture:field-health` (#1967) The captures page shows, for each source, the fare and leg fields that its parser gave before and now leaves empty: for the fares of the last 14 days, the share with no service number, no carrier, no fare name, no travel class and no flexibility, next to the share of the 90 days before, with a mark when a share went from below 10 % to 50 % or more (P3: a site change that removes one field gives a good capture and no failure, so no other mark shows it; P1: only the stored offers)
  - bahn.de fares with a service number in each of 40 fares of the older span and in none of 12 fares of the last 14 days show "bahn.de: no service number in 12 of 12 fares (before: 0 of 40)" with the mark; a field that a source never gives (empty in 40 of 40 fares before) shows no mark
- [ ] `capture:reparse-old` The status per source (`capture:health`) shows the count of good captures that an older parser version parsed, with "Reparse with version N": it parses the stored raw payloads of these captures again with the current parser (`capture:reparse-all` does it only for the failed captures) and shows the count of offers that each capture gave before and now (P3: a parser fix that reads a new field also fills the older fares; P1: only the stored payloads, no request to a travel site)
  - With 4 good bahn.de captures of parser version 1.3.0 and the current version 1.4.0, the row shows "4 captures of an older version"; after the reparse the 4 captures show version 1.4.0, their fares keep their price history, and the row shows no count

## 2. Rank offers by effective cost

- [x] `rank:base` (#10) Base formula (price + hours × hour_markup + transfers × transfer_markup), a breakdown per row, sliders with live re-ranking
  - A slider change re-ranks the list at once
  - Each row shows its breakdown ("€90 price + €62 time + €30 transfers = €182")
- [x] `rank:door` (#94) Door-to-door: access and egress places (time and cost), airport buffer, baggage fee
  - A flight and a train for the same trip compare on door-to-door effective cost
- [x] `rank:time` (#140) Night travel bonus or penalty, time-of-day penalty windows, different hour values for day and night
  - A departure before the early limit shows its penalty in the breakdown
- [x] `rank:risk` (#172) Penalties for short transfers and self-transfers, flexibility tiers, mode preference
  - A transfer shorter than N minutes shows its penalty in the breakdown
- [x] `rank:filters` (#179) Hard filters: max transfers, max duration, earliest departure, latest arrival, excluded carriers and modes
  - An excluded offer does not show in the list; a penalized offer shows lower
- [x] `rank:profiles` (#228) Named profiles, one default, an override per trip, "save as profile"
  - A change of the profile re-ranks the trip page at once
- [x] `rank:stale` (#274) Offers that were not captured again for N hours get a "stale" flag and an optional penalty
  - A stale offer shows "stale, price may have changed"
- [x] `rank:currency` (#382) Prices in other currencies: exchange rates to EUR in Settings, and the score uses the EUR value
  - A CHF offer ranks by its EUR value, and its breakdown shows the CHF price and the EUR price; an offer in a currency with no rate shows "no rate for CHF"
- [x] `rank:class` (#689) Travel class: a 1st class or business fare gets a comfort value per hour in the score
  - With a 1st class bonus of €10 per hour, the breakdown of a 1st class fare of 3 hours shows "1st class −€30"
- [x] `rank:travelers` (#761) Group trips: the score compares the fares by the price per traveler; the ingest stores the travelers of each capture, and a capture for another number of travelers than the trip shows a warning
  - A bahn.de capture for 2 adults into a trip for 2 shows "€180 for 2 travelers" next to the price of €90 in the breakdown; a capture for 1 adult into that trip shows "These offers are for 1 traveler; the trip has 2"
- [x] `rank:tradeoff` (#892) For an offer that is not the best, the trip page and the compare view show the hour value at which it becomes better than the best offer
  - "1 h 40 min faster, €25 more: better above €15 per hour (yours: €20)" shows on the compare view and in the expanded row of the trip table
- [x] `rank:profile-page` (#1006) Profiles on their own page, and hour tiers of the time cost (owner request)
  - A profile is made and edited on `/profiles/new` and `/profiles/{id}` with no trip; with the rates €4/h, €8/h after 4 h and €15/h after 8 h, a trip of 9 h has a time cost of €63 (4 × €4 + 4 × €8 + 1 × €15)
- [ ] `rank:return-fare` A return fare (one price for both ways, as most flights have) compares with the single-way offers of a round trip, by the rule of the decision #1036 (owner request)
  - The owner sees on one page if a return flight or the best two single-way offers (train, bus or flight) cost less in effective cost
- [x] `rank:source-fee` (#1141) The booking fee of each source in the effective cost
  - With a Trainline fee of €1.50, the same ICE from bahn.de at €90 and from Trainline at €89 ranks the bahn.de fare first
- [x] `rank:profile-compare` (#1205) The best offer of a trip for each profile on the trip page, with a link that selects the profile
  - With the profiles Cheap (€2 per hour) and Comfort (€30 per hour), the trip page shows "Best by profile": Cheap gives the slow Flixbus at effective €97 and Comfort the ICE at effective €121
- [x] `rank:overnight` (#1255) A transfer in the night costs a hotel night: an amount and a minimum of night minutes in "Advanced"
  - With a hotel cost of €80, a transfer in Köln from 23:40 to 06:10 shows "overnight stop in Köln €80" in the breakdown, and a direct night train ranks above it
- [x] `rank:arrive-by` (#1293) An "arrive by" time per trip: the hours of wait before it cost a wait value per hour, and the later offers do not show
  - With "arrive by" 14:00 and €5 per hour, an ICE that arrives at 10:30 shows "wait 3 h 30 min before 14:00 €17.50" in the breakdown
- [x] `rank:co2` (#1415) The CO₂ of each offer in the effective cost: an estimate per traveler from the mode and the hours of each leg (kg per hour for train, bus and flight in "Advanced"), and a price per kg with the default €0, so that the score does not change until the owner sets it (P2)
  - With €0.10 per kg and 90 kg per flight hour, a flight of 2 h shows "CO₂ 180 kg €18.00" in the breakdown, and the expanded row of each offer shows its CO₂
- [x] `rank:filtered` (#1451) The trip page shows how many offers the hard filters (`rank:filters`) and the table filters hide, for each filter, and the best hidden offer, so that a forgotten filter does not hide the best offer (P2: the ranking stays explainable)
  - With "max transfers 1", the trip page shows "3 offers hidden by the filters: 2 by max transfers, 1 by excluded carrier", and "best hidden: €84 effective, below the best shown €97"; "show them" shows the 3 rows greyed with the filter that hides each
- [x] `rank:station-change` (#1499) A transfer to another station of the same city (for example Paris Nord to Paris Gare de Lyon) has its own minimum transfer time and penalty in "Advanced" (default €0), and the row shows "change of station", also in split tickets (`trip:split`)
  - An itinerary whose leg arrives at one station and whose next leg departs from another station gets the mark and the penalty in its breakdown; a transfer at the same station gets neither
- [x] `rank:bags` (#1524) The bags of each flight fare in the baggage fee (`rank:door`): the parsers store the checked bags that a fare includes and the price of its first checked bag (kiwi.com `bagsInfo`); a fare with an included bag gets no baggage fee, a fare with a known bag price gets that price, and the other flights keep the flat fee of "Advanced"
  - A kiwi.com flight with `includedCheckedBags` 1 shows "bag included" and no baggage cost in its breakdown; a flight with a first bag tier of €25.06 shows "baggage €25.06"; a manual flight offer keeps the flat fee
- [x] `rank:carrier` (#1529) A markup or discount per carrier in "Advanced" (default none, P2), next to the exclusion of `rank:filters`: an itinerary with one or more legs of that carrier adds the amount once, and the breakdown shows it
  - With "Ryanair +€15", a Ryanair flight shows "carrier €15" in its breakdown and ranks lower; the other offers do not change
- [x] `rank:mode-hours` (#1566) An hour value per mode (train, bus, flight, ferry) in "Advanced" (default empty: the hour value of the profile, so the score does not change, P2): the minutes of each leg cost the hour value of its mode, the airport time (`rank:door`) costs the flight hour value, and the transfer and access minutes keep the hour value of the profile
  - With €15 per hour and "train €8 per hour", a train of 6 h shows "time €48.00" in its breakdown, and a flight of 1 h 30 min with 150 min of airport time keeps "time €22.50" and "airport time €37.50"
- [ ] `rank:reservation` (#1618) A train leg with a mandatory seat reservation that its fare does not include (the parsers store it where the payload tells it: bahn.de, NS International, Trainline) adds a reservation cost per leg in "Advanced" (default €0, so the score does not change, P2), and the row shows "reservation required"
  - With "reservation €10 per leg", a TGV leg with a mandatory reservation shows "reservation €10.00" in its breakdown and the mark on its row; a fare that includes the reservation and an ICE with an optional reservation do not change
- [x] `rank:calibrate` (#1708) An hour value that fits the owner's bookings: for each booked journey (`trip:booked-fare`), the range of hour values at which its itinerary was the best offer of its trip at the time of the booking (the prices of the price history at `booked_at`, as `rank:tradeoff` computes the hour value of a row); the profile page shows the range that fits the most bookings, with "Use this hour value" (P2: a suggestion, the owner chooses; nothing changes by itself)
  - A booking that cost €20 more than the best offer to save 1 hour, and a booking that took 1 hour more to pay €40 less, give "Your bookings fit an hour value from €20 to €40 (2 of 2 bookings)"
- [x] `rank:fallback` (#1771) A transfer with no later connection: for each transfer of an itinerary, the trip page looks for a later captured itinerary of the same trip that also departs the transfer place after the planned departure and arrives at the destination on the same day; with none, the row shows "no later connection captured" and a penalty in "Advanced" adds to the score (default €0, P2; P1: only the captured itineraries of the trip, the app fetches nothing)
  - A trip with A → X → B (departs X at 21:00) and no captured itinerary that departs X after 21:00 shows "no later connection captured" on the row, and a penalty of €30 makes its score €30 higher; when an itinerary that departs X at 22:00 is captured, the mark and the penalty go away
- [x] `rank:berth` (#1806) A night value per berth: the night travel adjustment (`rank:time`) of a night-train fare depends on its berth (seat, couchette, sleeper; the ÖBB Nightjet tiers of the parser, and the other parsers where the payload tells it): one adjustment per berth in "Advanced" (default empty: the night adjustment of the profile, so the score does not change, P2), and the breakdown shows the berth
  - With "couchette −€40" and "seat €0", the Couchette fare of a Nightjet connection scores €40 better than the night adjustment of the profile gives, and its breakdown shows "night in a couchette −€40.00"; a day train does not change

- [x] `rank:transfer-place` (#1849) A markup per transfer place in "Advanced" (default none, P2), as `rank:carrier` does for a carrier: each transfer at a named station or airport (for example Brussels-Midi or Paris CDG) adds the amount, a negative amount is a discount, and the breakdown shows the place and the amount
  - A markup of €20 for Brussels-Midi adds €20 to each itinerary with a transfer there, and the breakdown shows "transfer at Brussels-Midi +€20"
  - With no markup set, the score of each offer does not change
- [x] `rank:best-range` (#1934) The range of hour values in which the best offer of the trip stays the best (the other weights of the profile do not change), as `rank:tradeoff` computes the hour value of the other offers: the trip page shows it next to the best offer, so that the owner sees if a small change of the hour value changes the choice (P2: the ranking stays explainable)
  - With the hour value €20, a best offer that stays the best from €12 to €35 per hour shows "best from €12 to €35 per hour"; a best offer with no upper limit shows "best from €12 per hour"; a trip with one offer shows no range
- [x] `rank:best-at-price` (#1963) Each other row of the trip page shows the price at which it becomes the best offer with the active profile and the other prices unchanged (its price less the difference of the effective costs), for example "best at €74 or less", next to its break-even line of `rank:tradeoff` (P2: the ranking stays explainable; it tells the owner the target price of `trip:target` for that offer); a row whose price must drop to €0 or less, or a fare with no rate (`rank:currency`), shows no such line
  - With the best offer at an effective cost of €140 and an ICE at €90 with an effective cost of €156, the expanded ICE row shows "best at €74 or less"; the line uses the same shown rows as the break-even lines (the page filters and the source filter count)
- [ ] `rank:currency-fee` A card fee for a price in another currency than EUR in "Advanced" (a percent, default 0 %, P2): the score adds the percent of the EUR value (`rank:currency`) of each fare in another currency, and the breakdown shows the fee on its own line (the cost that a bank card adds to a payment in CHF or GBP)
  - With a fee of 2 %, a fare of CHF 100 at a rate of 0.95 EUR adds €1.90 to the score and the breakdown shows "currency fee +€1.90"; a fare in EUR, or a fee of 0 %, does not change the score
